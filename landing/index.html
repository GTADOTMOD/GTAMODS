23:07 Under the hood

Your key stays on your PC.

  1. In the game

    The mod

    Shows the screens and takes your key presses. It never holds your key.

  2. On your PC

    The companion

    Starts with the game, keeps your wallet encrypted and signs your trades.

  3. On Solana

    The market

    pump.fun for coins, Jupiter for swaps. Every trade is simulated before it can be sent.

  • Encrypted with your password

    Scrypt and AES-256-GCM. Your wallet starts locked each time, with no key in memory until you unlock it.

  • Practice comes first

    A new wallet starts in Practice, where nothing is sent. Real money is a switch you turn on yourself, with your password.

  • Confirm screens start on Cancel

    Each one lasts 30 seconds and works once, so a double press can't send a second trade.

  • Limits you set

    A cap on every trade and on slippage. Lowering one is instant. Raising one asks for your password.

  • You can always leave

    Export your private key to any Solana wallet, or reset RUGR and start again.

  • Story mode only

    Our mods stay out of GTA Online. Take the mod files out before you go online.

01:40 Questions

Straight answers.

Is GTAMOD part of Rockstar Games?

No. GTAMOD is an independent studio. We aren't made by, affiliated with or endorsed by Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive. You need your own copy of the game.

Can I use RUGR in GTA Online?

No. It's for story mode only. Script Hook V, which our mod needs, turns off the game's anti-cheat, so take the mod files out before you play online.

Do you ever hold my money or my key?

No. Your wallet is an ordinary Solana wallet, encrypted on your own PC under your password. We can't reach it, and we can't reset the password for you, so export your private key and keep it safe.

Is it live?

Yes, in early access. Download RUGR free for GTA V on PC. A new wallet starts in Practice, where nothing is sent, and real money is a switch you turn on yourself.

What do I need to run it?

Just GTA V on PC. RUGR Setup installs the rest with one click: the three free mods RUGR runs on, Script Hook V, ScriptHookVDotNet and iFruit Jailbreak, and borderless mode. The download page shows how.

Is this a good way to make money?

Treat it as a game with real stakes. Most memecoins lose their value, and trades can't be undone. Only put in what you can lose. Nothing here is financial advice.
